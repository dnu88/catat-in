Kaswise · Mobile

Click-through recreation of the live Kaswise mobile app — Indonesian-language personal finance tracker. Tap the splash to enter login, login to land on Beranda, then explore the four tabs and the center "+" to manually log a transaction. All components reuse the system tokens from colors_and_type.css.

Source · mobile/app + mobile/src · Expo Router · NativeWind · Phosphor icons

The iOS frame is for showcase only — the real app ships on iOS and Android via Expo. Components are visual recreations; persistence and AI are mocked. See ui_kits/mobile/README.md.
